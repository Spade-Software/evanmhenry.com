# Content and design

This is Evan M. Henry's personal portfolio and writing space. Use Evan M. Henry or EvanMHenry.com when naming the website/property. Preserve intentionally authored visible copy rather than globally replacing names.

Keep copy short, direct, curious, and concrete. Focus on what Evan builds and is interested in: software, design, jiu-jitsu, MMA, lifting, health and fitness. Attention to detail means getting pixels and animations right. Avoid sentimental lifestyle taglines, invented accomplishments, and the word “tinkering.” Social links stay quiet.

Use existing CSS tokens and components before adding new ones. The intro/project rail is the chosen layout. Project marks use white variants. Inline Spade links use the 18-pixel slightly rounded icon without a border; hover changes the underline, not a surrounding box. Do not restore portrait rotation. Preserve the staggered fade/rise/blur entrance and reduced-motion support. Keyboard focus stays visible; images and links need suitable accessible names.

Writing needs an explicit title, description, readable article width and stable route. Source content belongs in Git. Don't add a CMS or empty feed just to scaffold a section. Check article links and 404 behavior in the export. Drafts stay on a branch until Evan wants them published.

Prototypes compare layout, hierarchy, density or motion against the real stylesheet. They are disposable references, not production components. See `docs/prototype.md`.
